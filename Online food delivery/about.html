Our Chef's



Head Chef Arjun Mehra
With over 12 years of experience in the culinary industry, Chef Arjun leads our kitchen with dedication and innovation. He specializes in multi-cuisine dishes including Italian, Indian, and Continental. His philosophy is simple — “Great food is made with love and fresh ingredients.” Under his leadership, our kitchen maintains the highest standards of quality, taste, and presentation.

Chef Ananya Sharma
Pastry & Dessert Specialist Chef Ananya specializes in desserts and bakery items. With 7 years of experience in confectionery arts, she creates delicious cakes, pastries, and sweet dishes that complete your dining experience. Her attention to detail and presentation makes every dessert visually appealing and unforgettable in taste.

Chef Rahul Verma
Chef Rahul is an expert in fast food and fusion dishes. With 8 years of experience, he ensures that every pizza, burger, and pasta is prepared with perfect taste and consistency. His creativity adds a unique twist to our signature dishes. He focuses on maintaining kitchen discipline, hygiene, and customer satisfaction.
