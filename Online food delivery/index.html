Our Speciality



Samosa is a classic, authentic Punjabi-style delight, featuring a crispy, flaky, deep-fried shell filled with a savory mixture of spiced, fluffy potatoes, green peas, and fresh coriander. Served hot with tangy tamarind and spicy mint chutneys, this golden-brown snack delivers the perfect crunch and traditional flavor in every bite.



Pav Bhaji is a rich, buttery, and spicy Mumbai-style street food experience, featuring a perfectly mashed blend of vegetables (potatoes, tomatoes, peas, cauliflower) cooked on a hot griddle with spices and generous dollops of butter. It is served with soft, toasted, butter-soaked bread rolls (pav), garnished with fresh coriander, onions, and lime.



Pizza features a hand-tossed, airy crust made fresh daily, topped with a signature rustic tomato sauce, 100% mozzarella cheese, and unique, high-quality toppings. We elevate the experience with gourmet ingredients like truffle oil, artisanal cheeses, or savory marinated proteins



Dosa is a masterclass in texture and flavor, designed to offer the ultimate South Indian dining experience. It is crafted from a perfectly fermented, slightly tangy batter of rice and lentils, ensuring a light, airy structure that is easy to digest.
